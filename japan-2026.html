Our journey began in Tokyo, a city that perfectly blends the ultra-modern with the traditional. The sheer scale of everything takes a few days to get used to.


The Tokyo skyline at dusk

One of the highlights was visiting Senso-ji temple in Asakusa. The food stalls leading up to the temple were amazing. We tried everything from fresh melon pan to savory yakitori.

"Wherever you go, go with all your heart." - Confucius

Incredible omakase sushi
Golden Pavilion in Kyoto

After a few days, we took the Shinkansen down to Kyoto. The contrast was immediate. While Tokyo felt like stepping into the future, Kyoto felt like a gentle embrace from the past.

We couldn't get enough of the matcha-flavored treats. From ice cream to traditional tea ceremonies, the earthy, rich flavor was a daily staple. You simply can't leave Kyoto without trying authentic matcha.

The bamboo forests in Arashiyama were the perfect way to wrap up the trip. Walking through the towering green stalks early in the morning before the crowds arrived was an unforgettable experience.

Overall, a 10/10 trip. Highly recommended!
