This is not an exhaustive list of trips, but rather a space to document some of my highlights. Currently at 30 countries and counting! If you have any recommendations I'd love to hear them. Some destinations I'd love to visit next are Antarctica, New Zealand, The Galapagos Islands, and China.

These links don't go anywhere yet. I'd like to flesh them out when I find time.

  • East Africa Safari: Tanzania & Kenya
    September 2026

    Arusha, Ngorongoro Crater, Northern Serengeti, Naboisho Conservacy, and Nairobi - plus a proposal!

  • Weebing out in South Korea
    July 2026

    From figurine hunting in Seoul to recreating When Life Gives You Tangerines on Jeju Island.

  • Getting surrounded by cows in The Azores
    April 2026

    Roamed Sao Miguel and Pico Island, though this whole archipelago is very underrated!

  • My first time in South America: Brazil, Argentina, Paraguay
    June 2025

    Spent a lot of time in Brazil - Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Manaus, and Iguacu. Explored the Amazon rainforest from a jungle lodge. Stuck my head into Argentina and Paraguay on day trips.

  • Running around Turkey
    November 2024

    Spent a week in Istanbul before exploring Cappadocia, Antalya, and Selcuk.

  • The Philippines and Japan
    June and July 2024

    Explored Manila and Boracay before heading to Japan for ~3 weeks.

  • Meeting Santa in Finland
    April 2024

    Flying into Helsinki and taking the Santa Clause Express up to Lapland in the Arctic Circle.

  • Date night in London and Spain
    October 2023

    Visiting my long distance girlfriend in London and taking her to Seville for a long weekend.

  • Quick loop through Western Europe
    March 2023

    Hopping through Hungary, Austria, and the Czech Republic

  • Road trip around Morocco
    May and June 2022

    Boys trip going through Marrakech, Merzouga, Fes, Chefchaounen, and Casablanca.

  • Western Europe Grad Trip
    Summer 2019

    A 3 week trip through Greece, Italy, and Switzerland, with a couple bonus days in Spain and France to close things out.

  • First excursion to Asia: Hong Kong and Thailand
    November 2018

    Working out of Hong Kong for a week along with the other interns, with a long weekend in Chiang Mai.
